# Dye Studio direction-of-shift check (2026-11-17)

Scope: qualitative hue-direction check of the released predictor against practitioner statements of starting color, dye and result. No numbers were fitted and the predictor was not changed. Photos could not be viewed (Reddit blocked in the browser pane, DGCR bot check, fetch failures), so every row is text-only evidence.

Method: released studio.js predictor run in daylight at radius 0.7 with the 13 inventory dyes on Innova Star and Innova Champion profiles. Hue in degrees, value in percent.

| Reported case (source) | Model | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Blue iDye Poly on yellow Star goes deep green (DGCR blue-disc thread) | Star: hue 85, value 16 (very dark olive-green). Champion: hue 196, value 30 (blue-gray) | Star direction plausible but darker than reported; Champion shifts blue, not green |
| Blue and purple iDye on orange Star come out grayish-black and maroon (DGCR true-colors thread) | orange + Poly Blue, Star: hue 82, value 15 (near black) | Consistent |
| Red on yellow gives orange (several guides) | yellow + Crimson, Star hue 13; Champion hue 8 | Consistent |
| Red on blue gives murky purple-brown (Pine Tree guide) | blue + Crimson, Star: hue 208, value 22 (dark navy). Champion: hue 303, value 30 | Star mismatch; Champion closer |
| Blue on pink gives electric blue (Spike It worm dye, different medium) | pink + Poly Blue: hue 239 to 243 | Consistent in direction |

Open mismatches (do not change without controlled evidence): Champion blue-on-yellow lacks the green shift; Star red-on-blue lacks purple-brown. Both are single-source or guide-level statements, so no coefficient change is justified.
