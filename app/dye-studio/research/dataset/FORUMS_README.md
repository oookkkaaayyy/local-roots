# Forum/community dye evidence (summary)

96 observation records in forums.json (many are partial: unstated fields marked "not stated"). Target of 150 NOT met.

## Qualitative rules (with sources)
- Plastic uptake: Star/GStar/Champion/ESP/Opto/Fuzion/Lucid/Gold Line/Neutron/Prodigy 400 good; Pro and EchoStar middling; DX, Pro-D, D-Line, Retro, K3, Prodigy 300/350 poor (dgcr "instructions", "what plastics take dye", Reddit wiki rating chart, UDisc, Prodigy blog). Soft/Gateway SSS/Elastic Performance dye poorly or blur.
- Champion/translucent: slower (about 24 h vs 12 in bed methods), paler but holds lines longer; Star takes fast, fades faster.
- Base color: cannot dye lighter; white best. Orange base darkens iDye (blue gave gray/black, purple gave maroon). Red over yellow gives orange. Yellow/pink bases give unexpected results.
- Layering: darkest first; black/red/orange/yellow overlay with little shift; blue over red (or reverse) shifts hue; blue+yellow makes green; complementary colors go brown/muddy.
- Black: first dip purple (Rit), darkens with repeated dips; iDye Poly black leans blue, more vivid.
- Dye type: Jacquard iDye Poly (not regular iDye) and ProChem disperse work; Rit new formula reported weak (older bottles, Sunshine Orange, Scarlet, Royal Blue, Black worked; Fuchsia failed). Rit DyeMore used.
- Heat: hot (steaming, not boil; 110-130F reports for hot dip, 120F cure, 90F bed) penetrates faster; cold slows; overheating warps. Acetone/alcohol carriers penetrate fast.
- Fading: heat and sun drive migration/blur; freezer after dye reported to help; lines widen over years.
- Fluorescent/UV/glow: very little hard data. Neon ProChem pink stood out; neon yellow looked dull; glow plastic takes dye with less saturation; vendor says six "UV-reflective" colours. No blacklight measurements found.
